Recipient + Thing: The Arrow Diagram

Verbs like geben, schenken, zeigen, bringen, leihen, schreiben often take two objects: the recipient in the dative (to whom?) and the thing in the accusative (what?). Their word order depends on whether they are nouns or pronouns.

The 3 Word-Order Rules
CombinationOrderExample
2 nounsDative → AccusativePeter schenkt der Freundin ein Spiel.
1 pronoun + 1 nounPronoun firstPeter schenkt ihr ein Spiel. / Peter schenkt es Nadia.
2 pronounsAccusative → DativePeter schenkt es ihr.

Practice

Exercise

Put the sentence parts in the right order: *Peter lends his friend the bike.*

Give each entry its place: 1 for the first.

Right answer: 1. Peter, 2. leiht, 3. dem Freund, 4. das Fahrrad.

With two nouns, the dative (*dem Freund*) comes before the accusative (*das Fahrrad*).

Solution

Right answer: 1. Peter, 2. leiht, 3. dem Freund, 4. das Fahrrad.

With two nouns, the dative (*dem Freund*) comes before the accusative (*das Fahrrad*).

Exercise

Choose the right object order with pronouns.

  1. Peter gives it (das Buch) to her (Mina). → Peter gibt ….
  2. Lukas shows it (den Ball) to Peter. → Lukas zeigt ….
  3. Nadia writes him (Lukas) a card. → Nadia schreibt ….
Solution
  1. Peter gibt es ihr.
  2. Lukas zeigt ihn Peter.
  3. Nadia schreibt ihm eine Karte.

A single pronoun comes before a noun (ihn Peter, ihm eine Karte). With two pronouns, accusative comes before dative (es ihr).

Exercise

Type both objects in the right order.

  1. Peter gives the teacher (der Lehrer) the notebook (das Heft). → Peter gibt ….
  2. Lukas brings it (das Buch) to him (Peter). → Lukas bringt ….
  3. Mina gives her (Nadia) the ball (der Ball). → Mina gibt ….
Solution
  1. Peter gibt dem Lehrer das Heft.
  2. Lukas bringt es ihm.
  3. Mina gibt ihr den Ball.

Remember: 2 nouns = Dat → Acc; 1 pronoun = pronoun first; 2 pronouns = Acc → Dat.

Exercise

When both objects are pronouns (such as es and ihr), the dative pronoun always comes first.

Right answer: The statement is false.

False! With two pronouns, **accusative comes before dative**: *Peter gibt **es ihr**.*

Solution

Right answer: The statement is false.

False! With two pronouns, **accusative comes before dative**: *Peter gibt **es ihr**.*
