## Context: the page “Demonstrative & Indefinite Pronouns”

Use dieser, welcher, jeder, man, jemand, niemand, etwas, and nichts accurately in context.

### Which Gift? — dieser, welcher, jeder

Peter and Nadia are in the Lindenberg bookshop: *"**Welches** Buch gefällt Mina?" — "**Dieses** Buch hier!"* The words **dieser** (this), **welcher** (which), and **jeder** (every) take the exact endings of the **definite articles** (*der/die/das/den/dem*).

dieser / welcher / jeder Follow the 4-Case Color Table:
- Case | Masculine / Neuter | Feminine / Plural
- Nominative | dieser / dieses | diese / diese
- Accusative | diesen / dieses | diese / diese
- Dative | diesem / diesem | dieser / diesen

Vocabulary (German – English): Key Indefinite Pronouns (Always 3rd Person Singular!)
- man – one / people in general. Example: In Lindenberg fährt man viel Fahrrad. – In Lindenberg people cycle a lot.
- jemand – someone / anyone. Example: Hat jemand meinen Schlüssel gesehen? – Has anyone seen my key?
- niemand – nobody / no one. Example: Niemand ist zu Hause. – Nobody is at home.
- etwas – something / anything. Example: Möchtest du etwas trinken? – Would you like something to drink?
- nichts – nothing. Example: Ich habe nichts vergessen. – I haven't forgotten anything.

### Exercise demonstrativ-wahl

- Task: Choose the right form of dieser, welcher, or jeder.
- Answer format: choose a word for each gap
- How the app counts mistakes of form: capital letters wrong, accents wrong, hyphen wrong, apostrophe wrong, punctuation wrong
- Items (start → solution):
1. Which backpack (der Rucksack, acc.) do you buy? → Welchen Rucksack kaufst du?
2. I like this book (das Buch, nom.). → Dieses Buch gefällt mir.
3. Peter helps every friend (der Freund, dat.). → Peter hilft jedem Freund.
- Explanation: *dieser, welcher, jeder* carry the exact endings of *der/die/das* (*-er, -e, -es, -en, -em*).
- How to help: the app checks word by word against the solutions above and names mistakes of form (e.g. accents) with the child's words. Do not give the solution: ask about the rule (ending, gender, tense) or about a similar sentence of the page. If the app takes a version of the child's as wrong that you think is right, tell the child: the app knows only the versions above.

### Exercise indefinit-luecken

- Task: Fill in man, jemand, niemand, etwas, or nichts.
- Answer format: fill in the gaps in German
- How the app counts mistakes of form: capital letters wrong, accents wrong, hyphen wrong, apostrophe wrong, punctuation wrong
- Items (start → solution):
1. Here you (people in general) can buy fresh bread rolls. → Hier kann man frische Brötchen kaufen.
     - Mann: The indefinite pronoun *man* is lowercase with a single *n*.
2. Nobody knows the answer. → Niemand kennt die Antwort.
3. Is someone in the room? → Ist jemand im Zimmer?
4. I see nothing on the desk. → Ich sehe nichts auf dem Schreibtisch.
- Explanation: *man, jemand, niemand* refer to people; *etwas* and *nichts* refer to things. All take the *er/sie/es* verb form.
- How to help: the app checks word by word against the solutions above and names mistakes of form (e.g. accents) with the child's words.
