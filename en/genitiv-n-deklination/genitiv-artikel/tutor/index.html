Context: the page “Genitive Case & Prepositions”

Use the genitive case (des, der, eines, einer) for possession and after wegen, trotz, während, and statt.

Whose Bike Is That? — Row 4 Completes the Table

With the genitive (whose?), our 4-Case Color Table is complete! The genitive expresses possession and belonging (das Fahrrad des Bruders) and follows key prepositions: wegen, trotz, während, statt.

Picture: Complete 4-Case Color Table with Nominative, Accusative, Dative, and the new Genitive row: des/eines + -(e)s (m/n), der/einer (f), der/keiner (pl). Caption: Row 4 (Genitive): Masculine and neuter take des/eines + -(e)s on the noun; feminine and plural take der/einer/keiner.

Remember: When -s vs. -es — and What About Names?

Masculine and neuter nouns usually add -s (des Lehrers, des Regens). Short one-syllable nouns or words ending in s, ß, x, z add -es (des Tages, des Hauses). Proper names take -s before the noun: Peters Zimmer, Minas Gitarre.

Example: The Four Genitive Prepositions in Lindenberg

wegen (because of): Wegen des Regens fährt Peter mit dem Bus. · trotz (despite): Trotz der Kälte trainiert Mina draußen. · während (during): Während des Unterrichts macht Peter Notizen. · statt (instead of): Statt eines Buches kauft er ein Spiel.

Exercise genitiv-luecken

  • Task: Fill in the genitive article and noun (watch for -(e)s on masculine/neuter nouns).
  • Answer format: fill in the gaps in German
  • How the app counts mistakes of form: capital letters right with a hint, accents wrong, hyphen wrong, apostrophe wrong, punctuation ignored
  • Items (start → solution):
  1. Because of the rain (der Regen), we stay at home. → Wegen des Regens bleiben wir zu Hause.
    • des Regen: In the genitive, masculine Regen needs the -s ending: des Regens.
  2. That is the jacket of the sister (die Schwester). → Das ist die Jacke der Schwester.
    • der Schwesters: Feminine nouns do not take -s in the genitive: der Schwester.
  3. During the weekend (das Wochenende), Peter visits Nadia. → Während des Wochenendes besucht Peter Nadia.
  4. Despite the cold (die Kälte), Lukas rides his bike. → Trotz der Kälte fährt Lukas Fahrrad.
  • Explanation: Masculine and neuter form the genitive with des/eines + -(e)s on the noun; feminine and plural use der/einer/keiner with no -s.
  • How to help: the app checks word by word against the solutions above and names mistakes of form (e.g. accents) with the child's words. Do not give the solution: ask about the rule (ending, gender, tense) or about a similar sentence of the page. If the app takes a version of the child's as wrong that you think is right, tell the child: the app knows only the versions above.

Exercise genitiv-besitz-check

  • Task: How does Peter correctly refer to the room of his friend (mein Freund, m)?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ Das ist das Zimmer meines Freundes. – Correct! Possessive meines (masculine genitive) + noun Freundes (+ -es).
    • ✗ Das ist das Zimmer meinem Freund. – meinem is dative. Direct possession without a preposition uses the genitive: meines Freundes.
    • ✗ Das ist das Zimmer meines Freund. – The masculine noun is missing its genitive -es ending: meines Freundes.
  • Solution: Das ist das Zimmer meines Freundes.
  • Explanation: In the genitive, both the determiner (des/meines) and masculine/neuter nouns take -(e)s.
